## Words in the design

For texts you write into the design or the generated interface. Text the user gave, or that is
already there, stays word for word unless they ask. Write in the project's language, with its own
letters and one form of address (Turkish: sen or siz, as the project does).

- **Say the thing.** Headings hold a noun that names it; buttons are a verb and its object ("Randevu
  al", not "Keşfet", "Daha fazla", "Hemen başla"). A line that would fit another company unchanged
  goes out or gets a fact.
- **Invent no proof:** no made-up customers, quotes, ratings, percentages, awards or years. Sample
  data inside an app screen is fine: uneven, local (₺, 24 saat) and consistent, without the stock
  names (Acme, Sarah J., Ahmet Yılmaz).
- **Cut these patterns in any language** (Turkish forms in brackets):
  - contrast ("not X but Y") [X değil, Y; sadece X değil, aynı zamanda Y]; two fragments ["Az menü.
    Çok müdavim."]; negation runs [Kuyruk yok. Bekleme yok.]
  - question hooks and answers [Hazır mısınız? Peki ya…?]; openers [Günümüzde…, …dünyasında];
    invitations [… ile tanışın, gücünü keşfedin, bir adım öteye taşıyın, artık çok kolay]
  - empty praise: seamless, effortless, powerful, robust, elevate, unlock, empower, leverage,
    next-level [kusursuz, sorunsuz, zahmetsiz, eşsiz, benzersiz, yenilikçi, güçlü, akıllı çözümler,
    yeni nesil, uçtan uca, lider]; praise of oneself [samimi, özenle, tutkuyla, gerçekten, sadece]
  - riders that explain nothing [… sağlayarak, … sunarak]; vague sources [uzmanlara göre]; colon
    reveals; three of everything; a closing aphorism or a pun in the footer
  - em dashes, exclamation marks, emoji, all caps, Title Case in Turkish sentences.
- **Small texts too:** an error names the field and the fix ["Telefon 10 haneli olmalı"]; an empty
  state says what will appear ["Henüz sipariş yok. İlk sipariş burada görünür."]; a confirmation
  repeats the button ["Randevun alındı"].
- Do not swap a banned word for its synonym: use a fact or cut the line. Read it aloud once.
